→ THE THREAT

"Each post owns its own picture. The radar at P-04 sees a contact the IR at P-05 has already classified, but they never speak. Adversary movement falls between the gaps — and is reconstructed only after the fact, in incident reports."

OUR RESPONSE

Drishti fuses radar, EO/IR, SIGINT, acoustic, and orbital streams across the entire frontage — not at a back-end command centre, but at the edge — into a single picture that the operator at any post sees in full.

Suraksha holds the substrate sovereign. Every fused track is signed, audit-able, and reconstructable from the sensor up. The picture is live, not forensic.

Nirnaya surfaces options, not dashboards. When a contact crosses, the operator sees what to do — with provenance, confidence, and counterfactual on every choice.

"2.4 million square kilometres of EEZ. State and non-state vessels operate in the gaps. Detection is not the bottleneck — identification is. By the time a contact is named, it has already done what it came to do."

Vyooha coordinates a heterogeneous mesh — UAS, USV, satellites, shore radars — that persists through the IOR's vast gaps. Drishti fuses RF, acoustic, EO across all of them.

Suraksha holds the data inside India — including the satellite passes. Foreign hosting of Indian maritime telemetry is not on the table.

Counter-Swarm

Detect, classify, decide, and defeat — single drones to coordinated multi-axis swarm attacks. Layered, sub-4-second end-to-end. The defining threat of this decade, addressed as a system.

Mission CodeMIS-03 · COUNTER-UAS
DomainAIR · ALL

→ THE THREAT

"Cheap drones. Coordinated. Multi-axis. Existing radars miss them. Existing EW jams the friendlies along with the hostiles. The defining failure mode is indecision — too many options, too little time, no provenance to defend any choice."

OUR RESPONSE

Detection is the easy part. Decision under tempo is the hard part. Counter-swarm is a System-of-Systems problem — and it is exactly what we exist to solve.

Drishti fuses radar, EO, acoustic into one classified track per drone. Vyooha coordinates layered effectors — EW, kinetic, friendly UAS — without jamming the asset it is defending. Nirnaya surfaces three options to the operator in under 1.6 seconds, each with provenance.

Suraksha holds the audit trail. After every engagement, the operator can show — to a court or a court-martial — exactly why each decision was made.

→ THE THREAT

"Single-platform autonomy is brittle. The link drops, the GPS fails, the lead is lost — and the mission collapses. Worst of all: the operator has no idea until the silence becomes long enough to notice."

OUR RESPONSE

Vyooha distributes initiative. Every node carries a copy of the mission grammar — declarative, composable, ROE-encoded. The lead is elected, not assigned, and re-elects when lost.

The mesh persists through 30% node loss. Coverage zones reshape automatically. The operator sees the loss reflected in the picture in seconds — never as silence.

ROE is enforced in code, not in policy. A node will refuse an action that violates its rules, and explain — auditably — why.

What we don't do

FIG. ω / SCOPE

Capabilities are defined as much by what they exclude as what they include. We are an opinionated company.

The list to the right is what we have declined to build, on principle. If your procurement is for any of these, we are not the right partner — and we will tell you so in the first conversation.

What follows is not a hedge. It is the company's identity, expressed in refusal.

  • → 01We do not build autonomous lethality without a human in the loop. The operator commits the action — never the model.
  • → 02We do not host Indian operational data on foreign clouds. No exceptions, including for "convenience."
  • → 03We do not ship black-box models. Every recommendation carries provenance and counterfactual, or it is not surfaced.
  • → 04We do not build offensive cyber. Our system is for defence — sensing, coordinating, deciding — not for breaching.
  • → 05We do not sell to jurisdictions we cannot operate alongside. India and verified allies. The list is short and deliberate.
